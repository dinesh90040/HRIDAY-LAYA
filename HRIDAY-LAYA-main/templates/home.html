Understanding Your Heart

What is the Heart?

The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood throughout your body. It is located in the chest, slightly to the left, and is about the size of a fist. The heart is responsible for delivering oxygen and nutrients to your tissues and removing carbon dioxide and waste products.

Why is Heart Health Important?

Maintaining a healthy heart is crucial for overall well-being. A healthy heart ensures proper blood circulation, which supports all bodily functions. Poor heart health can lead to conditions like heart disease, stroke, and high blood pressure, which are leading causes of death worldwide.
